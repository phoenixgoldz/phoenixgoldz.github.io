Support

Help for Eucalyptus Edge: MegaBlast Mayhem and other PhoenixGold Game Studios projects.

Before reporting a problem

Restart Steam, verify the game's installed files, confirm Windows and your controller drivers are current, and note the game version shown on the F2 diagnostics screen.

Include in a report

Please include the game version, what you expected to happen, what happened instead, the steps that reproduce the issue, and any relevant screenshots or diagnostic details.
