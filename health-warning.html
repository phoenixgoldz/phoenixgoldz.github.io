Health Warning

Eucalyptus Edge: MegaBlast Mayhem

Photosensitive Seizure Warning

Some visual effects in Eucalyptus Edge: MegaBlast Mayhem include flashing lights, rapid visual transitions, explosions, screen shake, and bright particle effects. A very small percentage of people may experience seizures when exposed to certain flashing lights or visual patterns.

Accessibility Options

MegaBlast Mayhem includes accessibility settings that allow players to reduce or disable flashing visual effects at any time from the in-game Accessibility or Graphics settings.

Stop Playing Immediately If You Experience

  • Dizziness
  • Altered vision
  • Eye or muscle twitching
  • Disorientation
  • Loss of awareness
  • Involuntary movements or convulsions
  • Nausea or severe discomfort

General Health Recommendations

  • Play in a well-lit room.
  • Take regular breaks.
  • Avoid playing when tired or fatigued.
  • Sit a comfortable distance from your display.
  • If you have a history of epilepsy or photosensitive seizures, consult your physician before playing.
