Player Safety

Health Warning

Health and photosensitivity information for Eucalyptus Edge: MegaBlast Mayhem.

Photosensitive Seizure Warning

Some visual effects in Eucalyptus Edge: MegaBlast Mayhem include flashing lights, rapid visual transitions, explosions, screen shake, and bright particle effects. A very small percentage of people may experience seizures or other adverse reactions when exposed to certain flashing lights or visual patterns.

Accessibility Options

MegaBlast Mayhem includes visual and accessibility settings intended to reduce visual intensity. Players can adjust relevant effects and display options from the in-game settings menus.

Stop Playing Immediately If You Experience

  • Dizziness
  • Altered vision
  • Eye or muscle twitching
  • Disorientation
  • Loss of awareness
  • Involuntary movements or convulsions
  • Nausea or severe discomfort

General Health Recommendations

  • Play in a well-lit room.
  • Take regular breaks.
  • Avoid playing when tired or fatigued.
  • Sit a comfortable distance from your display.
  • If you have a history of epilepsy or photosensitive seizures, consult a medical professional before playing.

This page is provided as general player-safety information and is not a substitute for professional medical advice.
