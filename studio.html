PhoenixGold Game Studios is an independent studio creating the world of Verdantia, one game at a time.

Our first commercial Steam release, Eucalyptus Edge: MegaBlast Mayhem, turns the annual MegaBlast Festival into a local multiplayer arena battler. It’s built around quick decisions, expressive fighters, and a shared screen full of stories.

We care about responsive play, a world with its own identity, and the fun of getting everyone into the same game.
