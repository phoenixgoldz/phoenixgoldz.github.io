Faefera pre-alpha support

Faefera is still in active development and does not yet have a public Steam page or trailer. Current playtesting is PC-only and is being built around both keyboard-and-mouse and gamepad input. When reporting a demo or development-build issue, include the build/version, the input device you were using, what you expected to happen, and the steps that reproduce the problem.

MegaBlast Mayhem

Restart Steam, verify the game’s installed files, confirm Windows and your controller drivers are current, and note the game version shown in diagnostics before reporting a problem.

Include in any bug report

  • Game and build/version.
  • What you expected to happen.
  • What happened instead.
  • Steps that reproduce the issue.
  • Keyboard/mouse or gamepad model, when input is relevant.
  • Relevant screenshots, video, crash information, or diagnostics.
