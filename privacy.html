Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 31, 2026

PhoenixGold Game Studios respects your privacy. Eucalyptus Edge: MegaBlast Mayhem, its expansions, updates, and downloadable content do not operate a separate PhoenixGold player-account system and do not directly collect names, postal addresses, payment details, or similar personal information from players.

Games and downloadable content

This Privacy Policy applies to PhoenixGold Game Studios games and related content, including Eucalyptus Edge™, MegaBlast Mayhem™, the Padmara Expansion, all current or future DLC, updates, patches, visual upgrades, story additions, characters, arenas, and related downloadable content.

Steam services

The game may use Steamworks features including achievements, Steam Cloud, the Steam overlay, controller support, ownership verification, and DLC installation or entitlement checks. Information processed through those services is handled by Valve under Steam's own privacy terms and account settings.

Local game data

The game may store settings, progress, unlocks, achievements, DLC-related state, and save data on your device. When Steam Cloud is enabled, eligible save data may be synchronized through Steam.

Website security and administration

This website is maintained for informational and promotional purposes. Visitors are not authorized to modify, upload, replace, delete, or otherwise alter any portion of the website. Unauthorized attempts to access administrative functions, interfere with hosting, alter files, bypass security controls, or disrupt operation may be investigated and may result in access restrictions or appropriate legal action.

Children's privacy

PhoenixGold Game Studios creates family-friendly games and does not knowingly request personal information directly from children through its games or website.
