Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 27, 2026

PhoenixGold Game Studios respects your privacy. Eucalyptus Edge: MegaBlast Mayhem does not operate its own account system and does not directly collect names, addresses, payment details, or other personal information from players.

Steam services

The game uses Steamworks features including achievements, statistics, cloud saves, and the Steam overlay. Information processed through those services is handled by Valve under Steam's own privacy terms.

Local game data

The game may store settings, progress, unlocks, and save data on your device. When Steam Cloud is enabled, eligible save data may be synchronized through Steam.

Website data

This website is hosted through GitHub Pages. PhoenixGold Game Studios does not use this site to create player accounts or sell personal information.

Children's privacy

The game is designed to be family-friendly and does not knowingly request personal information from children.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact options on the Support page.
