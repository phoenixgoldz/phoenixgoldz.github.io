Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 21, 2026

PhoenixGold Game Studios respects your privacy. This website does not operate its own user-account system, and PhoenixGold Game Studios does not sell personal information collected through this site.

Website data

This website is hosted through GitHub Pages. PhoenixGold Game Studios does not use this site to create player accounts, process game purchases, or collect payment information. Third-party services you choose to visit from this site may apply their own privacy policies.

MegaBlast Mayhem & Steam services

MegaBlast Mayhem may use Steamworks features such as achievements, statistics, cloud saves, and the Steam overlay. Information processed through those services is handled by Valve under Steam’s own terms and privacy practices.

Faefera development builds

Faefera is currently in pre-alpha development and does not yet have a public Steam page. The game is being designed around local player progress, settings, inventory, creature, and world-save data. Player and creature names are intended to use a child-safe in-game name filter; they are game data, not a public chat or social-network feature.

Local game data

PhoenixGold games may store settings, progress, unlocks, customization choices, saves, and similar gameplay data on your device. Platform cloud-sync behavior, where offered, is governed by the platform providing that service.

Children’s privacy

PhoenixGold games are designed to be family-friendly. The website and games are not intended to ask children to publicly disclose personal information through an in-game social or chat system.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact options on the Support page.
