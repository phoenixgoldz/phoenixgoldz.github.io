The studio

The studio behind MegaBlast Mayhem

PhoenixGold Game Studios is Trevor Hicks, working out of Salt Lake City. The studio develops and supports MegaBlast Mayhem, a complete local multiplayer arcade game built around readable action, responsive controls, and memorable characters.

MegaBlast Mayhem grew from a university classroom prototype into the studio's first commercial Steam release. Its annual festival introduces players to Verdantia through fast local matches, expressive characters, and region-themed arenas.
