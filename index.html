What the studio builds toward

Three things every game has to earn

It reads in one frame

A player should know who they are, where they are, and what is about to kill them, without being told.

It feels right on the first input

Movement, bombs and hits are tuned before anything is added on top. Feel is not a polish task.

It belongs to Verdantia

Every fighter, region and sound comes from one world, so the next game already feels familiar.
