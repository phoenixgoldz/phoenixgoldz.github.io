Play Your Way

Designed for the whole couch

Readable menus, multiple input methods, flexible difficulty, and clear player identification make it easy to start a match and stay focused on the action.

  • Keyboard and controller navigation
  • Four bot difficulty levels
  • Separate music and sound-effect volume, defaulting to a quiet 40%
  • Windowed, borderless, fullscreen, and resolution options
  • Five color modes: Default, Deuteranopia, Protanopia, Tritanopia, High Contrast
  • Every player also carries a distinct shape — circle, triangle, square, diamond — so seats stay readable without relying on color
  • No quick-time events

Project History

From classroom prototype to commercial release

MegaBlast Mayhem began as a university project in 2021 years before its commercial release. The original concept was created by Trevor H. and co-developed with classmate Calvin B. during an introductory JavaScript and Object-Oriented Programming course.

What started as a classroom prototype for a classing 1993 bomberman using the newly learnd java skills evolved into a full PhoenixGold Game Studios' first commercial game and the first playable title set in Verdantia!

The festival ends with one promise: “The adventure continues in Eucalyptus Edge.”
