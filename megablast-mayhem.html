Welcome to Verdantia

One festival. Five unforgettable regions.

Ancient forests, luminous caverns, lively harbor paths, frozen mountain trails, and the blazing outback come together for the annual MegaBlast Festival.

MegaBlast Mayhem introduces Verdantia, its heroes, and its regional identity before the adventure continues in Eucalyptus Edge.

Playable Heroes

Meet the Festival Competitors

Eight distinctive fighters bring the personality of Verdantia to every match. Their pixel-era tournament appearances connect directly to their larger journey in Eucalyptus Edge.

Koda

A courageous koala guardian from Eucalyptus Summit who fights to protect his home and friends.

Ripper

A powerful Tasmanian devil brawler who thrives in the center of explosive chaos.

Kiri

A bold kookaburra competitor whose fearless spirit brings bright energy to every arena.

Banjo

A clever sugar glider rogue whose speed and confidence make him difficult to corner.

Atlas

A proud emu warrior whose reach, precision, and commanding presence make him a formidable rival.

Echo

A resourceful platypus fighter shaped by the frozen beauty and danger of Frostpine Ridge.

Wren

A fearless kangaroo boxer whose explosive strength makes him one of Verdantia's toughest competitors.

Sonia

The mysterious white tigress champion awaiting anyone strong enough to reach the festival finale.

Accessible Competition

Designed for readable, flexible local play

MegaBlast Mayhem supports keyboard and controller play, scalable presentation, adjustable audio, and player-identification options designed to keep four-player matches readable.

  • Keyboard and SDL2-compatible controller support
  • Windowed, borderless, fullscreen, and multi-monitor presentation
  • Resolution scaling and readable interface typography
  • Independent music and sound-effect controls
  • Colorblind-friendly player identification with persistent symbols
  • Player identity carried through rings, bombs, blast accents, HUD, and results

Project History

From Classroom Prototype to Commercial Game

MegaBlast Mayhem began as a university project approximately four years before its commercial release. The original concept was created by Trevor Hicks and co-developed with classmate Calvin Bryant during an introductory JavaScript and Object-Oriented Programming course.

What started as a classroom prototype evolved into PhoenixGold Game Studios' first commercial game and the first title set in the world of Verdantia.

The festival story concludes with Sonia's challenge, celebrates the tournament, and ends with one promise: “The adventure continues in Eucalyptus Edge.”
